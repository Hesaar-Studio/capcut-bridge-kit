# TikTok Raw Preset Style

Designed for high-engagement vertical short-form reels and TikToks.

## Visual Specs
- **Font**: Inter Bold (bundled in `assets/fonts/Inter-Bold.ttf`)
- **Placement**: Low-center under the subject's face (`y: -0.32` normalized)
- **Background Box**: None (raw clean cut)
- **Shadow**: Crisp 45% alpha black drop shadow with 5px distance
- **Colors**:
  - Primary text: `#FFFFFF`
  - High-impact keywords: `#FFD700` (Yellow) or `#00F0FF` (Cyan)
- **Animation**: Quick 0.25s Pop In, no outro delay
